Introduction

Welcome to the official documentation for BuildPCBs.com. This guide will help you get started, master the editor, and understand the core concepts behind the platform.

What is BuildPCBs.com?

BuildPCBs.com is an AI-native, browser-based IDE for schematic design, positioned as the 'Cursor for Hardware Engineers.' It's an intelligent co-pilot that automates the creation of complex schematic blocks directly from text prompts. Instead of manually placing components and wiring them together, you can simply describe the circuit you need, and our AI will generate a production-ready design for you on an interactive canvas.

Core Philosophy

Our core philosophy is to accelerate the hardware design process by handling the repetitive, boilerplate work that consumes countless engineering hours. By automating schematic creation, we empower engineers, students, and hobbyists to move faster, experiment more freely, and focus on what truly matters: innovation and building great products.

Getting Started

Follow these simple steps to experience your first "magic moment" with BuildPCBs.com and generate a schematic in seconds.

1

Register and Log In

Create your free account and log in to access the main editor interface.

2

Use the AI Co-designer

On the left side of the editor, you'll find the **AI Co-designer** sidebar. This is where you'll interact with the AI. Click inside the text input field.

3

Enter a Prompt

Type a description of the circuit you want to create. For example:

"Generate a support circuit for an STM32 microcontroller"
4

Watch the Magic

Press Enter or click the "Generate" button. The AI will analyze your request, select the necessary components (like decoupling capacitors, reset circuits, and oscillator crystals), and draw a complete, professionally arranged schematic directly on the canvas.

The Editor: A Feature Guide

The BuildPCBs.com editor is a powerful, interactive canvas. This guide breaks down its core features into easily scannable cards, helping you master the interface quickly.

Canvas Navigation & Interaction

Pan Canvas

Hold Spacebar and drag with your mouse.

Zoom

Use your mouse's scroll wheel to zoom in and out.

Select Component

Click on any component to select it.

Move Component

Click and drag a selected component.

Delete

Select an item and press the Delete key.

Context Menu

Right-click a component for more options.

Advanced Features

Inspect Components

Hover over a component to see its properties and the AI's design rationale in the sidebar. Click the component to lock the view.

Manual Wiring

Press the W key to enter "Add Wire" mode. Click two component pins to create a new connection.

Project Management

Saving and loading your work is simple and seamless. Here’s how to manage your projects effectively.

Saving Your Work

Click the **Save Project** button. If the `Project ID` field is blank, a new project is created with a unique ID. Subsequent saves update the existing project.

Loading a Project

Click **Load Project** and enter a `Project ID` to load a saved design back into the editor.

The Dashboard

All your saved projects are accessible from your personal dashboard. Navigate to /dashboard to see a list of your projects, where you can open, manage, or delete them.
